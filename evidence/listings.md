## 1. Chase Apartments, Blacksburg VA
url: https://www.apartments.com/chase-apartments-blacksburg-va/1cxxjw6/
address: 421-461 Roanoke St E, Blacksburg VA
sources read: 6 screenshots + the link, merged
pricing: per bed. $894 a bed, 4 bedroom unit, $3,576 whole unit
income used: $5,000 a month after tax
commute: drive, 0.5 miles, Virginia Tech

breakdown, their share
  rent                894
  utilities            70
  parking              10
  commute               5
  renters insurance    15
  true monthly cost   994

advertised share      894
gap                   100 a month   (994 - 894)
annual gap           1200           (100 * 12)
burden advertised      18 percent
burden true            20 percent

what the app caught that the listing did not say
  - priced per bed, not per apartment. Whole unit price put in the
    rent box with the share split four ways.
  - parking has a section on the listing with no price in it
  - heating type never stated. Worth up to $69 a month.
  - air conditioning type never stated. Worth up to $15 a month.
  - "subject to change without notice" flagged in the lease wording
  - total unresolved spread: up to $394 a month
