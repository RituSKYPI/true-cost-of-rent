      What is actually there

      Real buildings from Google, with no rent attached because Google does not publish rent. Open one, screenshot its pricing, then run it through "Check a place" for the real number.

        Every cost is computed in code from what you entered. The language model reads listings and writes explanations. It never produces a number.

        Electricity prices: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Table 5A, 2024. Typical area rents: U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Fair Market Rents. Travel times and places: Google Maps Platform. Utility consumption and the fee ranges are estimates and are labelled as such.

        What this collects

        Your income, your rent figures and your shortlist stay in your browser. They are never sent to the server and never stored. When you analyse a place the server records one line: which of three bands the result fell into, and whether the model was used. No rent, no income, no address, no identifier, nothing that could be traced back to you.

        Screenshots you upload are sent to Google's Gemini API to be read, and a copy is cached so the same screenshot is not read twice. Addresses you type into the commute box go to the Google Maps Routes API. Neither is tied to you.
